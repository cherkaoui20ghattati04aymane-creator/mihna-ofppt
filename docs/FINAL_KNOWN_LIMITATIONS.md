# Phase 12 known limitations

- The authenticated private production workflow requires the owner to keep or open a local signed-in session. Synthetic registration and data changes use isolated workspaces.
- Gemini is deliberately disabled during recording and disposable write workflows. The application labels local matching and interview output. Live Gemini was exercised in earlier phase reports. The opt-in standalone live probe is not part of the default suite.
- The catalog includes verified remote and public-sector opportunities. It still lacks a verified physical Marrakech listing. Direct employer links remain available for manual browsing.
- SMTP, paid search, external submission and public deployment were not exercised. Applications can be prepared and manually tracked.
- The responsive sweep checks that routes render and the document fits. It does not imply every interaction was repeated at all three widths.
- The recorded CV uses French text and lists Arabic as a language. Complex-script PDF shaping was not separately verified.
